Beyond the obvious of chatgpt, there are a huge number of awesome AI tools that can help with your work.

Knowledge work often involves a four step process:
1. Gather inputs - do the research and get the information you need
2. Process those inputs - read them, understand how they affect the project
3. Output - produce the report, diagram, spreadsheet etc.
4. Feedback - using your work on the output, feed this back into the input stage to further refine the output.

Many use AI tools for the output phase of the lifecycle. But, in my experience, I find that the output of AI tools is this soup of average ideas hoovered up from the internet.
Instead, I prefer to use AI tools to assist me with steps 1 and 2.

* Elicit

### Process inputs
* NotebookLM

### Output refinement
* Napkin AI

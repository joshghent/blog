As a person who grew up on the internet and rode the wave of online services coming and going, I recently realised that I had a huge digital footprint.
My email resembled the M25 at rush hour, my home was bombarded with newsletters, my phone number rang constantly with scam callers. It was bad and clearly drastic steps needed to be made.

Here is what I did...

## Goals
The overarching theme of this project was to obtain more privacy in my digital life. I began to see the impacts on seemingly ordinary people who, for just a moment, had the spotlight shone on them. I also observed the digital drag net that large corporations hauled through the online sphere and how that data is used for manipulation and profit.

For the record, I'm not a security researcher, journalist or activist. Nor do I expect said digital spotlight to shine on me. Whilst taking these steps initially appeared to me as inflating my sense of self-importance, I reasoned "why should I have all my data out there?". The curtains analogy always speaks to me in this regard. It goes like this... in your bedroom you likely have windows, and at night you cover said windows with curtains, blinds or maybe both. People can guess what you do in the bedroom (which is beyond the purview of this discussion). But, you still obscure what is happening because you don't want people to see in.

This was largely my thinking on this privacy project.

## Areas of improvement
